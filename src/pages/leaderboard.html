How points work

Consistency beats intensity

Points are earned for showing up, not for lifting the most. The weekly and monthly boards reset; all-time keeps counting. Coaches verify PBs and goals.

+20Every gym check-in (one per day)
+15Every class or coached session attended
+25A personal best logged with your coach
+50A goal ticked off at your check-in
+2Each reaction you receive on the wall
+50Bring a mate to a session
