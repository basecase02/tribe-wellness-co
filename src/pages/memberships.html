Questions

Good to know

Is there a free trial?

Yes. A 7 day free trial pass is available through the online sign-up portal, and the 28 Day Kickstarter gives you unlimited gym and classes for four weeks.

Can I access the gym 24/7?

Yes. Gym and FIFO memberships include 24/7 access. Staffed hours are Monday to Thursday 5.30am–7pm, Friday 5.30am–12pm and Saturday 8am–12pm.

How big are the semi-private sessions?

Semi-private personal training is capped at a small group of four to five members per coach, so you get individual attention with the energy of training alongside others.

What is the Ladies over 60 program?

A specialised semi-private training program for women over 60. Certified trainers guide safe, effective workouts designed to improve fitness, health and overall wellbeing, from $69 per week.

How does the FIFO membership work?

Unlimited access to all classes and gym facilities during your off-weeks and days off, from $34.50 per week.

How do I join?

Sign up online through the member portal on the Join page in a couple of minutes, or contact us about personal training, over 60s and FIFO options.
